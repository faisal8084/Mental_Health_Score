    Under the hood

    How the prediction works

    A trained regression model looks for patterns across thousands of student responses to estimate wellbeing.

    ①

    You share your habits

    Screen time, sleep, stress, study and activity levels — thirteen data points captured in one quick form.

    ②

    The model scores them

    A FastAPI service running a trained scikit-learn pipeline turns your inputs into a single 0–10 score.

    ③

    You get a clear gauge

    The result renders instantly as an animated dial with a plain-language read of what it means.

    About this tool

    Built for awareness, not diagnosis

    MindGauge is an educational demonstration of applied machine learning. It estimates a general wellbeing score from self-reported digital and lifestyle habits — it does not diagnose any mental health condition and is not a substitute for professional care.

    If you or someone you know is struggling, please reach out to a licensed mental health professional or a local support service.
